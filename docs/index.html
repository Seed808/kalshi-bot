How the bot picks
  • For every Kalshi game starting in the next 36 hours, it backs the side the sportsbooks give the better chance to win. If no sportsbook lists the game, it uses Kalshi's own prices instead, but only when they're tight enough to trust. Soccer picks are always a team to win, never a tie.
  • About 25 games in 100, a coin flip sends it to the underdog instead. If recent news mentions injuries for the favorite, it also takes the underdog.
  • A pick makes the slate only with at least a 55% win chance, or as a clear value underdog: priced by sportsbooks and returning at least 8% more than it costs, on average.
  • The slate holds up to 15 picks, highest win chance first, with about a quarter of the spots kept for value underdogs. At most 15 new picks a day. Each pick is a $10 paper bet at the Kalshi price; a contract pays $1 if your side wins.
  • Top Pick # ranks open picks by win chance. Best Bet # ranks them by value: win chance divided by what a contract costs, including Kalshi's fee. A 30% underdog at 25¢ can be a better bet than an 80% favorite at 85¢.
  • Favorites still lose often, and paying the favorite's price usually costs a little over time. This is for watching games, not a money-making system.

Win chances are the average of several sportsbooks' moneylines with their built-in margin removed. Picks are paper bets only. No real orders are ever placed, and nothing here is betting advice.
