How the bot picks
  • For every Kalshi game starting in the next 36 hours, it backs the side the sportsbooks give the better chance to win. If no sportsbook lists the game, it uses Kalshi's own prices instead, but only when they're tight enough to trust. Soccer picks are always a team to win, never a tie.
  • About 25 games in 100, a coin flip sends it to the underdog instead, so it isn't all favorites. If recent news mentions injuries for the favorite, it also takes the underdog.
  • Each pick is a $10 paper bet at the Kalshi price. A contract pays $1 if your side wins. At most 50 picks are open at once and 50 a day.
  • Favorites still lose often, and paying the favorite's price usually costs a little over time. This is for watching games, not a money-making system.
