How to read this board
  • A Kalshi contract pays $1 if your side wins and nothing if it loses. The price in cents is what one contract costs, so 29¢ means you pay $0.29 to maybe win $1.
  • Sportsbooks' chance is how likely several sportsbooks think that side is to win, with their built-in margin removed.
  • Avg. profit per contract is the sportsbooks' chance minus the Kalshi price minus Kalshi's fee. Positive means Kalshi is selling it cheap. Negative means it's overpriced.
  • The bot only makes a paper play when that number is at least ++3.0¢. Anything smaller is too close to call.
